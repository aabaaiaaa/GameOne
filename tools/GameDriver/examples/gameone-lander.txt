# GameOne: the lander and its mission, in a new world and three written for it, each played in turn.
# - A new world: the mission menu with the new mission; LAND ON THE MOON chosen, the stack on Home's
#   pad under its wide fairing, the mission's steps; the right-click menu on the booster, its legs
#   put out by it; launched straight up, the booster dropped, the engine cut and the fairing's foot
#   cone close by (the HUD hidden, F2), then the fairing jettisoned, its halves coming away.
# - The stack round the moon by day, 60 degrees short of the landing spot: the spot's marker over
#   the moon, and on the map (M); SPACE releases the lander, seen from the side.
# - The lander alone on its way down, its de-orbit burn just done (flown on from the release by
#   the tests' pilot, the upper stage left on its orbit above, as in the next): the retrograde hold, the spot's
#   marker as it nears, the right-click menu in flight putting its legs out (no autopilot here: it
#   comes down unbraked, and crashes).
# - Landed on the spot by day, its legs out and its doors shut: on the marker with the planet in
#   its sky; its legs and foot pads as close as the camera comes (F2); the right-click menu, "Open
#   bay doors", the doors opening and open as ramps, from round it; free look (the middle button)
#   up to the planet.
# Each world is played and then deleted by its row's markers ("Play: <its name>", "Delete: ..."),
# wherever it is. The camera's drags are undone before each world is left, as its heading carries
# on into the next. The pointer is parked over the view (350 200), clear of the panels, for the
# wheel's zoom and so the steering rings don't show. Run with GAMEONE_WORLDS set to a new, empty
# folder with only the lander's three worlds written into it:
# GAMEONE_WRITE_MOON_WORLD=<folder> dotnet test tests/GameOne.Tests --filter "FullyQualifiedName~Writes_the_lander_worlds"
# GAMEONE_WORLDS=<folder> dotnet run tools/GameDriver/drive.cs -- src/GameOne/bin/Debug/net10.0/GameOne.exe tools/GameDriver/examples/gameone-lander.txt --out shots
#
# 1. A new world: the mission from the pad.
0    until Menu = Worlds
1    click marker "New: Empty world"
0    until Menu = Missions
1.5  shot missions      # the mission menu: LAND ON THE MOON among the five
2    click marker "Mission: LAND ON THE MOON"
0    until Menu = None
1    move 350 200
3    shot pad           # the stack on Home's pad under its wide fairing; the briefing; the mission's steps
3.5  readings pad
4    tap f2             # the HUD hidden ...
4.5  scroll -2
5    rdrag 0 -30 350 200 # ... and a little lower, looking up at it
9    shot pad-stack     # the whole stack, the fairing over the upper stage
9.5  rdrag 0 30 350 200
10   scroll 2
10.5 tap f2
12   rclick marker "Flown vessel"
13.5 shot menu-booster  # the right-click menu on the booster: its name, "Deploy legs"
14   readings menu-booster
14.5 click marker "Action: Deploy legs"
15   move 350 200
0    until Legs = deployed within 10
1    shot legs-out      # the booster's legs out on the pad
3    tap space          # lit ...
3.1  tap z              # ... at full throttle, straight up
0    until Altitude >= 2500 within 90
0    tap space          # the booster dropped, over the thick air
0.2  tap x              # the engine cut: the stack on its way up with its engine off
1    shot booster-off
1.5  rdrag -262 0 350 200 # from the side, the sun behind the camera
2    tap f2
2.5  scroll 4           # close by: the fairing's foot cone over the upper stage
6.5  shot fairing-foot
7    scroll -3
10   shot fairing-side
10.5 tap space          # the fairing jettisoned
10.8 shot fairing-0
11.3 shot fairing-1
12   shot fairing-2
13   shot fairing-3
15   shot fairing-4
15.5 tap f2
16.5 shot fairing-hud
17   readings fairing
17.5 scroll -1
18   rdrag 262 0 350 200
20   tap escape
0    until Menu = Game
0.5  move 0 37
1    click 0 37         # Change world...
0    until Menu = Worlds
1.5  click marker "Delete: Empty world" # the world just played ...
3    click marker "Yes, delete it" # ... yes: the three lander worlds left
#
# 2. The stack round the moon.
4.5  click marker "Play: The lander: in orbit round the moon"
0    until Menu = Missions
1    click marker "Mission: LAND ON THE MOON" # the spot's marker is this mission's
0    until Menu = None
1.5  click marker "Add view"
2.5  click marker "Add view: Upper stage"     # a view of the stack (the upper stage with the lander on) ...
3.5  move -492 250
4    click -492 250     # ... flown: its view clicked
0    until Body = Moon within 10
1    move 350 200
1.5  scroll 5
2    rdrag 0 60 350 200 # looking down past it at the moon
6    shot orbit-stack   # the stack round the moon by day, the spot's marker over the moon
6.5  readings orbit-stack
7    tap f2
7.5  scroll 2
11   shot orbit-stack-near
11.5 tap f2
12   tap m              # the map
17   shot orbit-map     # the spot's marker on the moon, the orbit over it
17.5 readings orbit-map
18   rdrag 262 0 350 200
22   shot orbit-map-turned
22.5 rdrag -262 0 350 200
23   tap m
40   shot orbit-ahead   # on towards the spot, its marker on the ground ahead
40.5 readings orbit-ahead
41   rdrag -262 -60 350 200 # from the side, level with it, the sun behind the camera
45   shot release-before
45.5 tap space          # the lander released
45.8 shot release-0
46.5 shot release-1
47.5 shot release-2
49.5 shot release-3
52.5 shot release-4
53   readings release
60   shot release-5
60.5 rdrag 262 0 350 200
61   scroll -7
62   tap escape
0    until Menu = Game
0.5  move 0 37
1    click 0 37         # Change world...
0    until Menu = Worlds
1.5  click marker "Delete: The lander: in orbit round the moon" # the world just played ...
3    click marker "Yes, delete it" # ... yes: two left
#
# 3. The way down (two listed).
4.5  click marker "Play: The lander: on the way down to the moon"
0    until Menu = Missions
1    click marker "Mission: LAND ON THE MOON"
0    until Menu = None
1.5  click marker "Add view"
2.5  click marker "Add view: Lander"     # a view of the lander ...
3.5  move -492 250
4    click -492 250     # ... flown, falling
0    until Body = Moon within 10
1    move 350 200
1.5  scroll 6
2    tap r              # the retrograde hold: its bell turned down the way it falls
7    shot down-start
7.5  readings down-start
0    until Ground <= 700 within 120
0    rclick marker "Flown vessel"
1.5  shot menu-lander-flying # the right-click menu in flight: "Open bay doors", "Deploy legs"
2    click marker "Action: Deploy legs"
2.5  move 350 200
0    until Ground <= 400 within 60
0    shot down-400      # the spot's marker ahead as it nears
0.1  readings down-400
0    until Ground <= 150 within 60
0    shot down-150
0.1  readings down-150
0    until Ground <= 40 within 60
0    shot down-40
0    until State = Crashed within 30
1    shot down-crashed
1.5  scroll -6
3    tap escape
0    until Menu = Game
0.5  move 0 37
1    click 0 37         # Change world...
0    until Menu = Worlds
1.5  click marker "Delete: The lander: on the way down to the moon" # the world just played ...
3    click marker "Yes, delete it" # ... yes: one left
#
# 4. Landed (one listed).
4.5  click marker "Play: The lander: landed at the spot"
0    until Menu = Missions
1    click marker "Mission: LAND ON THE MOON"
0    until Menu = None
1.5  click marker "Add view"
2.5  click marker "Add view: Lander"     # a view of the lander ...
3.5  move -492 250
4    click -492 250     # ... flown, landed
0    until Body = Moon within 10
1    move 350 200
1.5  scroll 6
2    rdrag 524 0 350 200 # looking south, to the planet
2.5  rdrag 0 -120 350 200 # low, level with the ground
7    shot landed        # landed on the spot's marker by day, its legs out, its doors shut, the planet in its sky
7.5  readings landed
8    tap f2
8.5  rdrag 655 0 350 200 # round to its sunlit side, the sun behind the camera
13   shot legs-near     # its legs and foot pads, as close as the camera comes
13.5 rdrag 0 40 350 200
17.5 shot legs-near-high
18   rdrag 0 -40 350 200
18.5 tap f2
20   rclick marker "Flown vessel"
21.5 shot menu-lander   # the right-click menu on the lander: "Open bay doors", "Stow legs"
22   readings menu-lander
22.5 click marker "Action: Open bay doors"
23   move 350 200
24   shot doors-0       # opening: the doors swinging out, their panels unfolding
25.5 shot doors-1
27   shot doors-2
28.5 shot doors-3
0    until "Bay doors" = open within 10
1    shot doors-open    # open as ramps, onto the ground
1.5  readings doors-open
2    tap f2
2.5  rdrag 0 40 350 200
3    scroll -2          # back a little: the ramps reach well out
6.5  shot ramps-0       # the ramps from round it, a quarter at a time
7    rdrag 262 0 350 200
11   shot ramps-1
11.5 rdrag 262 0 350 200
15.5 shot ramps-2
16   rdrag 262 0 350 200
20   shot ramps-3
20.5 rdrag 655 -40 350 200 # south again, low
20.7 scroll 2
21   tap f2
22   mdrag 0 -25 350 200 # free look up, to the planet in its sky
26   shot look-planet
26.5 readings look-planet
27   mdrag 0 -25 350 200 # further up
31   shot look-up
31.5 quit
