# GameOne: the pad lights, at night. Picks Sentinel (at night when a new world starts: 07:30 at Home), and looks at the rocket on
# its pad: lit by the four floodlights, from four sides, so it casts several faint shadows, not one
# black one; the floor lights round the pad's edge, and the lights up the green tower. Then it
# climbs away (the lights and shadows follow the nearest lit place: from 1.5 km, none) and is
# restarted, back on the pad under its lights. Then quit. Each "until" waits for a reading.
# GAMEONE_WORLDS=<empty folder> dotnet run tools/GameDriver/drive.cs -- src/GameOne/bin/Debug/net10.0/GameOne.exe tools/GameDriver/examples/gameone-padlights.txt --out shots
0    until Menu = Worlds
0.4  click marker "New: Empty world"
0    until Menu = Missions
0.4  click marker "Site: Sentinel"
1.4  click marker "Mission: LAUNCH AND LAND" # from Sentinel
0    until Menu = None
0    until Site = Sentinel within 5
2    shot pad
2.3  rdrag 0 120                    # look down on it: the shadows round its foot
3.3  shot shadows
3.5  tap space                      # light the engine ...
3.8  tap z                          # ... at full throttle: away from the pad, out of its lights
0    until Ground >= 1500 within 40
0    tap x
1    shot far                       # (from far off: the pad's four lamps, points of light)
1.5  tap escape
2.5  move 0 -65
4    click 0 -65                    # Restart flight: back on the pad, under its lights again
0    until Menu = None
2    shot back
2.5  tap escape
3.5  click 0 109                    # Quit game
