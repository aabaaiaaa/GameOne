# GameOne: free play. Make a world, pick Free play (last in the mission menu), and launch the
# satellite launcher: no objectives in the mission panel, and no mission titles; a shot on the pad
# and one at 400 m.
# Run with GAMEONE_WORLDS set to a new, empty folder, so the world made has its preset's name (a
# new world's is made unique among those saved) and the driver's worlds stay out of yours. The
# world list's buttons are clicked by their markers, wherever they are.
# GAMEONE_WORLDS=<empty folder> dotnet run tools/GameDriver/drive.cs -- src/GameOne/bin/Debug/net10.0/GameOne.exe tools/GameDriver/examples/gameone-freeplay.txt --out shots
0    until Menu = Worlds
1    click marker "New: Empty world"
0    until Menu = Missions
1    click marker "Mission: FREE PLAY"
0    until Menu = None
0    until Mission has free within 5   # the free play mission, not another
2    shot pad           # the briefing, and an empty mission panel
2.5  tap space          # light the engine (at 75%) ...
2.6  tap z              # ... and full throttle, as the climb wants
0    until Altitude >= 400 within 60
0    shot climb         # no "target altitude": nothing is asked of it
1    quit
