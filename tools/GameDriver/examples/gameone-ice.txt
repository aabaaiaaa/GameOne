# GameOne: the sea ice, in the Austral Sea (the icy sea round the south pole, some 14 km from the
# pad: further than a script can fly, so the world starts there, at its local noon).
# Over the frozen sheet's edge from 150 m: the sheet (ice, not snow on land and not water), its
# ragged edge, the floes in the water beyond it and the band they make, tinted from far off;
# then lowered to about 30 m over the edge, and the floes near, a second apart (riding the waves);
# then the camera drawn far out, past the floes' 2.5 km, where they give way to the band's tint,
# and from further still, looking down on the sheet and the band round it.
# The world is written by a test (tests/GameOne.Tests/IceWorld.cs): one booster hovering 150 m
# over the sheet at its edge. Run with GAMEONE_WORLDS set to a new, empty folder with only that
# world written into it (its Play is clicked by its marker, by the world's name, so other saved
# worlds there would do no harm, but the driver's worlds stay out of yours):
# GAMEONE_WRITE_ICE_WORLD=<folder> dotnet test tests/GameOne.Tests --filter "FullyQualifiedName~IceWorld"
# GAMEONE_WORLDS=<folder> dotnet run tools/GameDriver/drive.cs -- src/GameOne/bin/Debug/net10.0/GameOne.exe tools/GameDriver/examples/gameone-ice.txt --out shots
0    until Menu = Worlds
1    click marker "Play: Sea ice: over the Austral Sea's edge" # the one saved world: the ice
0    until Menu = Missions
1    click marker "Mission: LAUNCH AND LAND" # a new flight on the pad, the booster still hovering over the ice
0    until Menu = None
1    move -600 328
1.5  click -600 328     # Add view ...
2    move -560 308
2.5  click -560 308     # ... of the booster over the ice (the only entry)
3.5  move -492 250
4    click -492 250     # fly it: click its view
0    until Altitude >= 100 within 10
2    shot edge-150      # over the sheet's edge from 150 m
2.5  rdrag 0 150        # look down at the edge
4    shot edge-down
4.5  rdrag 0 -150
5    rdrag 520 0        # and round the other way
6.5  shot edge-round
7    rdrag -520 0
7.5  down ctrl          # the throttle well under the hover: coming down ...
7.8  up ctrl
0    until Ground <= 42 within 30
0    tap z              # ... then full, to stop at about 30 m ...
0    until Vertical >= -4 within 10
0    down ctrl          # ... and back to about the hover
0.75 up ctrl
1.5  shot edge-30       # about 30 m over the edge
2    rdrag 0 120        # look down at the floes by the edge
3    shot floes-a       # twice, a second apart: they ride the waves
4    shot floes-b
4.5  scroll 6           # in closer
6    shot floes-close
6.5  rdrag 0 -120
7    scroll -40         # far out: past the floes' range, the band's tint
9    shot far-1
10   scroll -20
12   shot far-2
13   scroll -40         # further out still ...
14   rdrag 0 200        # ... looking down on the sheet and its band
16   shot far-down
17   quit
