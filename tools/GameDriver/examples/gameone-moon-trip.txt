# GameOne: the moon trip, from a low orbit round the planet to the moon's ground, in six worlds
# (the trip's starts, and moments captured from the trip flown by the tests' autopilots), each
# played in turn and deleted after.
# Each world has the satellite launcher's upper stage (flown from a view of its own, as an earlier
# flight's vessel) and a satellite on a round orbit 9 km over the planet, given the second small
# view: so from the moon there is a small view at the planet beside the main view there.
# - A low orbit, the moon targeted: the window shaded on the orbit ahead (90 s on), and the map
#   zoomed out step by step to its furthest.
# - The transfer burn done: the path bending round the moon in the map; then warped on to the
#   moon's sphere and slowed, a run of shots a second apart across the crossing (no jump); then
#   the map at the moon, the way in bending round it.
# - Arriving in the moon's sphere: the link-up over the stage's new small view (once the moon's
#   physics, kept from the last world, has been let go: it is made behind the link-up); the main
#   view at the moon beside the small view at the planet; switching to the satellite at the
#   planet and back (no link-up: both bodies' physics are made by then); the map round the moon,
#   the way in bending round it; levelled and turned, the planet behind the moon from beyond.
# - On the round orbit over the moon's poles: the loop and its readouts; the map at every zoom
#   from the nearest to the furthest; then warped on, a shot every 4 s, the stage passing behind
#   the moon (hidden by it).
# - On the way down, the de-orbit burn just done and the engine off (no autopilot here: it comes
#   down unbraked, and crashes), on the retrograde hold: the landing lights coming on in the
#   moon's night.
# - Landed at the landing spot by night: its lights on the ground; free look up and round (the
#   middle button) for the planet over the horizon.
# The worlds are written by a test (tests/GameOne.Tests/MoonTripWorlds.cs), oldest first in the
# order played; each is played and deleted by its row's markers ("Play: <its name>", "Delete: ..."), wherever it is. Run with GAMEONE_WORLDS set
# to a new, empty folder with only those six worlds written into it (the scripts' worlds alone):
# GAMEONE_WRITE_MOON_WORLD=<folder> dotnet test tests/GameOne.Tests --filter "FullyQualifiedName~Writes_the_moon_trip_worlds"
# GAMEONE_WORLDS=<folder> dotnet run tools/GameDriver/drive.cs -- src/GameOne/bin/Debug/net10.0/GameOne.exe tools/GameDriver/examples/gameone-moon-trip.txt --out shots
#
# 1. The low orbit.
