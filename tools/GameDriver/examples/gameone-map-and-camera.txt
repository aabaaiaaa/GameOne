# GameOne: the map, the camera, targets and nodes, from a low orbit flown by readings. LAND ON THE
# MOON's stack leaned east and put on the Prograde hold, the booster dropped, the engine cut at the
# apoapsis wanted and lit again from it until the periapsis clears the air; in the map the moon
# targeted by its orbit line and the AN and DN read; the camera put on the moon (zoomed in and
# turned until the landing spot shows), then on the landing spot, M for its chase view, and Reset
# camera; two nodes placed at the AN and the DN, the first dragged along its path (Node times
# before and after: the second moves with it); the Manoeuvre hold; a click on the heading ring
# (the rings' hold); then the camera put on the planet in the map, and quit (the world is saved).
# gameone-map-and-camera-carry-on.txt carries it on in the same folder and reads them back.
# The marks move round the screen as the stage goes round (the map follows it): each is clicked
# once it is clear of the panels and the booster's small view on the left.
