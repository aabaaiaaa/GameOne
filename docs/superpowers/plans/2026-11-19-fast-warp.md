# Fast Warp Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A doubling time-warp ladder up to ×16,384 whose rates above the physics' top rate move every vessel by formula, from event to event over the whole frame, with the physics brought back only as the warp drops.

**Architecture:** The low rates (¼ to `Scene.PhysicsTopRate`) stay today's loop of 1/60 s physics steps (`Flight.Advance`). Above them a frame is one advance by formula (`Flight.AdvanceByFormula`, in a new partial file `Flight.Warp.cs`): every vessel, the flown one included, is under formula, the frame is cut into windows of `Scene.FormulaWindowSeconds` and at the earliest event (a warp target, a sphere crossing, a drop's moment), collisions between vessels under formula are found over each window by looking back along both vessels' ways (`FormulaCollisions`), and nothing of Jolt's is stepped. The player's rate is one index into the ladder; a gate (a witnessed vessel flying in the air) refuses the formula's rates, and drops set the index down to the physics' top rate, never back up.

**Tech Stack:** C# (.NET 10), BRUTAL (Jolt physics, Vulkan, ImGui), xUnit (`tests/GameOne.Tests`, run by `tools/TestRun`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-fast-warp-design.md` (binding; read it with this plan).

## Global Constraints

- The ladder doubles all the way: ¼, ½, ×1, ×2, ×4 in the physics, then the formula's from the next doubling on, ×8, ×16, …, ×8,192, **×16,384**; where the physics stops is the measurements' call (Task 1), and the ladder is the same either way.
- The ladder's top, where the physics' rates stop, the averaging's rate and the formula's step sizes are each one number in `Scene` (the spec: "so the ladder's top, where the physics' rates stop, the averaging's rate and the formula's step sizes are each one number in `Scene`").
- One step a press, as today (the user: "Just one at a time"): holding a rate key does not step on. Task 3 (built as hold-to-step, bfc7585) is reverted.
- A warp to a point on the path (`FlightInput.WarpTo`) goes at the new top rate (the highest the gate allows).
- **The warp never climbs back by itself**: a drop lands at the physics' top rate and stays there; a rate key past the physics' top rate while it isn't allowed does nothing.
- **Crossing a sphere's edge doesn't drop it.**
- **Only one reason restricts the warp**: a witnessed vessel flying in the air (or low over the ground), shown on the HUD with the vessel's name, and in the readout. A witnessed vessel not yet at rest counts as flying.
- Witnessed (for the gate) = the flown vessel, each small view's, and the one the main camera is on.
- **The unwitnessed never bring the physics in, nor change the warp**, at any rate; one within `Scene.HandoverDistance` of a witnessed vessel is in the physics with it at the physics' rates, as today.
- **Nothing skipped**: a step of minutes never passes a vessel through the ground, the air's top, another vessel, a sphere's edge or the solar system's edge unseen.
- **No change to the physics' rates' behaviour**: flying at ×1 to ×4 is as today, and the flight tests that fly there pass unchanged (a test whose pilot asked for the old top rate, ×32, asks for `Scene.PhysicsTopRate`).
- **No hand-holding**: the warp dropping is information, never a prompt.
- The frame holds 60 fps at the top rate: ×16,384 reached (within 10%) at 60 fps paced in Release on a moon transfer and in the hundred satellites' world; the `Flight.Capacity` world (1,000 vessels) measured and reported, its target set from what is measured.
- The physics' rates stay full Jolt steps of 1/60 s of game time.
- CLAUDE.md holds throughout: game logic through `FlightInput`, tunables in `Scene.cs`, no test code in the game, everything in each body's frame (double where precision needs it), `Readout.cs` for what a script might fly by, comments say what and why (never history), files kept CRLF, builds with `--disable-build-servers -nodeReuse:false` and `dotnet build-server shutdown` when done, no backwards compatibility (tests whose outcome changes are changed to match).

## Decisions made for what the spec leaves to the builder

Each is binding on the tasks below.

- **The rate model:** `Flight._rate` stays the player's index into `Flight.Rates`, the only rate there is (no hidden "asked" rate above it). The gate (`Flight.WarpHeldBy`, the first witnessed vessel neither at rest nor above the air) only refuses the Faster key past `Scene.PhysicsTopRate`; drops set `_rate` to the physics' top index (`DropToPhysics`); nothing raises it but the keys and a warp to a point. The last drop is kept for showing (`Flight.LastDrop`, a `WarpDropped`).
- **Which drops exist** (`WarpDrop`): `Air` (a witnessed vessel's way into the air, a body with air), `Ground` (into reach of the ground, a body without), `SetMoving` (the flown vessel at rest launched, throttled up, staged, its legs or bay doors moved), `Steering` (a steering key: `FlightInput.Lean`), `Collision`, `NearMiss`, `Node`, `FuelOut`. Each comes `Scene.WarpDropLeadSeconds` (4 s of game time) ahead of what it is for, by cutting the frame there. Any vessel action (Launch, Stage, ToggleLegs, ToggleBayDoors) at a formula rate drops the warp whatever the vessel is doing, before the input is taken, so staging is always done in the physics (the spec's non-goal); the throttle and the rings do not drop it while flying.
- **A drop at the physics' rates** (the warnings' today) still goes to ×1, once each (unchanged `Flight.Mark`); a formula rate's collision or near-miss drop goes to the physics' top rate, once each other vessel.
- **A frame at a formula rate** is windows of at most `Scene.FormulaWindowSeconds` (8 s game), each a light step (`FormulaStep`): the vessels' rules, every vessel moved by formula to the window's end, crossings, the edge, the collisions under formula, the runs; no Jolt step, no `Unwatched.Update`, no `LookAhead`. A window is cut short at the earliest event: the warp target, a vessel's crossing, a drop's moment. The frame stops early once its real budget is spent, at a window's end.
- **Collisions under formula over a frame** (`FormulaCollisions`): looked for backwards over each window once every vessel has been moved to its end: each vessel's way across the window is the cubic Hermite through its states at the two ends (good to a few centimetres over 8 s of orbit); swept boxes (both ends, padded by its reach and the most its pull bows the way off the chord, a·T²/8) sorted along X; for each overlapping pair, the closest approach of their Hermite ways, and where that comes within their reach, the existing body-to-body solve (`Unwatched.Solve`'s, made shared as `FormulaContact.Touch`) over a `Scene.FormulaCollisionSeconds` stretch round it, from each one's Hermite state there. A vessel whose motion was remade inside the window (it touched, landed, crossed, staged) is left out of that window's pairs. The physics' rates keep `Unwatched.FindCollisions` as it is; on the drop, its window carries on from the last formula window's end (no reset).
- **The witnessed vessels' collisions and near misses at a formula rate** are asked once a window (`Collisions.AheadOfTheWarp`), for each witnessed vessel against every other at its body, over the window plus `Scene.CollisionHorizonSeconds` plus `Scene.WarpDropLeadSeconds`: pairs ruled out by distance against their closing bound, the rest flown ahead by the existing chord march (`Collisions.Closest`); a collision by their sizes, or with the near-miss setting on a near miss by today's rule, cuts the frame at its moment less the horizon and the lead, and drops the warp there.
- **Burns under formula:** above the air a stepped vessel's formula step is `Scene.FormulaBurnStepSeconds` (1 s; in the air it stays `Scene.FormulaStepSeconds`, 0.1 s), and a witnessed burning vessel's rules (its holds, its fuel) run every burn step, the step cut where the fuel runs out, so neither the thrust nor the aim is carried past what it was. The unwitnessed take their rules once a window, as now once a step.
- **Turning under formula** is the aim's (`Vessel.Steer`, at `Vessel.TurnRate`), the attitude following the aim as the formula has it now; at a formula rate the witnessed burner's aim turns every burn step, a coaster's every window.
- **Nodes under formula:** the flown vessel flies its nodes under formula (`FollowNodes`, the manoeuvre hold's `BurnLeft`), the unwitnessed still don't; a node coming up drops the warp once per node, at its time less half its burn and the lead.
- **Crossings at a formula rate:** a vessel on its orbit keeps its next crossing's time (`FormulaMotion.CrossesAt`, from `Patched.Legs`' crossing search, worked out as its orbit starts), and the windows are cut there so it is handed across at that moment; a stepped one is asked at each of its steps' ends, as the physics is asked at each step's.
- **The physics at a formula rate:** the worlds in use are kept (not stepped, so not released), each witnessed vessel's body is asked ahead every frame (`Worlds.Ahead`), `Worlds.Lag` is zeroed as the formula takes over, and `AskAhead`'s horizon is held to `Scene.PhysicsAheadSeconds × Scene.PhysicsTopRate`. `BringIn` does nothing at a formula rate (a vessel switched to, given a view or watched stays under formula until the drop); a link-up due at a formula rate asks its body ahead rather than making it.
- **What the HUD shows at the formula's rates:** everything as at ×1, only faster. The time line says `WARP 16384x`, held `WARP 4x  (held: Booster 1 flying in the air)`, and for `Scene.WarpDropShownSeconds` (3 s real) after a drop `WARP DROPPED to 4x: Booster 1 coming into the air`. The map, the small views and the paths draw as now.
- **The sea's waves and the eclipses** at those rates skip with the world's clock (drawn at the frame's time); the averaged light hides the sun's disc and with it the eclipses.
- **The averaged light** (above `Scene.AveragedLightRate`, ×256, eased over `Scene.AveragedLightEaseSeconds` real): one weight (`Flight.Light.Weight`, 0 to 1) handed to the renderer (`FrameInput.LightAveraged`, `FrameUniforms.Averaged`); with it, the sun's direct light falls as from straight overhead at `Scene.AveragedSunStrength` of its strength (an even dusk from pole to pole), no sun shadows, the sky drawn with the sun `Scene.AveragedSunElevationDegrees` over each view's horizon, the sun's disc, corona and flare gone. The pad and landing lights hold at their place's dark share of a day (`Sun.DarkShare`, sampled at `Scene.AveragedLightSamples` times of day).
- **The unwitnessed's simple staging:** both parts go on from the vessel's place and velocity, the bottom stage at its own place along the axis, each with its own stages, mass and fuel (delta-v by the numbers); a fairing's halves each pushed out at `Scene.FairingSeparationSpeed` on their sides and tumbling as the physics' do, clipping ignored.
- **Settling under formula:** afloat at once where it comes down within the splashdown's limit; over a seabed shallower than its plunge's reach it reaches the seabed at `Scene.FormulaSeabedSpeedShare` of its splash speed, judged there by the ground's rules (landed on it, or wrecked). Aboard a raft it is carried by the raft's pose at the world's time (`Raft.Pose`), the deck-relative place kept as it landed.
